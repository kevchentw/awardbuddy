Search award space across every date at once.

Award Buddy adds a search panel to airline and hotel booking sites. Pick your routes or hotels and a date range, and it checks every combination on the site for you, then puts the results in one sortable table and calendar.

What makes it different

Award search sites mostly let you check one route on one day. Award Buddy checks all your dates and routes in one go, on the airline's or hotel's own site.

Many dates, one table

Every origin × destination × date runs through the site's own search. Sort by date, duration or miles, and filter by cabin, stops or day of the week.

Live data from the source

Results come straight from the program's site, so you see what you could book right now. Nothing comes from a cached third-party database.

No server, no account

It runs in your browser with your own session on the airline's or hotel's page. Nothing is sent anywhere else, and it doesn't collect any data.

Calendar views

Calendar mode shows the lowest price per day for whole months. Hotel searches show points per night for each hotel, one color per hotel.

Flights and hotels

10 airline programs and 6 hotel programs, including partner awards, stopovers and Choice points at Preferred Hotels.

Links to booking

Most results link to the program's booking page for that flight or night, so you can go from a search to booking quickly.

Install

Install it as a userscript today. It updates itself automatically. A Chrome / Edge extension is coming soon.

Userscript (recommended)

  1. Install Tampermonkey or Violentmonkey.
  2. Open award-buddy.user.js and click Install.

Updates arrive on their own through Tampermonkey.

Install userscript

Chrome / Edge extension Coming soon

A one-click install from the Chrome Web Store and Edge Add-ons is on the way. The link will be posted here.

Until then, the userscript works in Chrome and Edge with Tampermonkey.

Then search

  1. Open a supported site from the list below. A button appears in the corner of the page: ✈ on airline sites, 🏨 on hotel sites.
  2. Click it to open the panel. If it asks for a session, log in or follow its Get session link.
  3. Enter your routes or hotels and dates, then click Search. Results appear as each request finishes.

Hotel search covers standard reward nights paid in points. Add hotels by name, city or airport, pick them from the page you're on, or type a hotel code (for example IHG TPEKM, Hyatt TYOPH). More hotel chains are planned.

Extra search modes

Air Canada: one-way with stopover
Enter stopover cities and stay lengths (for example 3-5, 7 days). Every city × stay length is searched for each route and date.
Japan Airlines: partner awards
Search JMB partner space on Alaska / Hawaiian, American, British Airways, Cathay Pacific, Qatar, Emirates, Finnair, Iberia, Qantas, Korean Air and more.
LifeMiles: carrier filter
All of Star Alliance, Smart Search, or one airline: Thai, Avianca + Gol, Aegean, ANA, Lufthansa, Singapore Airlines or United.
I Prefer: I Prefer or Choice points
Search Preferred Hotels & Resorts priced in I Prefer points, or the 300+ hotels bookable with Choice Privileges points.
