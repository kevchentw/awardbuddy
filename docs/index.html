Features

What makes it different

Award search sites mostly let you check one route on one day. Award Buddy checks all your dates and routes in one go, on the airline's or hotel's own site.

Many dates, one table

Every origin × destination × date runs through the site's own search. Sort by date, duration or miles, and filter by cabin, stops or day of the week.

Live data from the source

Results come straight from the program's site, so you see what you could book right now. Nothing comes from a cached third-party database.

No server, no account

It runs in your browser with your own session on the airline's or hotel's page. Nothing is sent anywhere else, and it doesn't collect any data.

Calendar views

Calendar mode shows the lowest price per day for whole months. Hotel searches show points per night for each hotel, one color per hotel.

Flights and hotels

11 airline programs and 6 hotel programs, including partner awards, stopovers and Choice points at Preferred Hotels.

Get started

Install in under a minute

Add the extension from the Chrome Web Store, or install the userscript. Both update themselves.

Chrome / Edge extension Recommended

  1. Open Award Buddy on the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Click Add to Chrome. In Edge, click Allow extensions from other stores first.

Chrome and Edge keep the extension up to date.

Add to Chrome

Userscript

For other browsers, or if you already use a userscript manager.

  1. Install Tampermonkey or Violentmonkey.
  2. Open award-buddy.user.js and click Install.

Updates arrive on their own through Tampermonkey.

Install userscript

01

Open a supported site

Pick one from the list below. A button appears in the corner of the page: ✈ on airline sites, 🏨 on hotel sites.

02

Open the panel

Click the button. If it asks for a session, log in or follow its Get session link.

03

Search everything

Enter your routes or hotels and dates, then click Search. Results appear as each request finishes.

Changelog

What's new

Downloads for every version are on GitHub Releases.

  • 1.9.0Sep 30, 2026

    Delta SkyMiles award search Latest

    • New airline program: Delta SkyMiles on delta.com. No login needed.
    • Delta's own flights and partners like Air France, KLM, Virgin Atlantic and Korean Air, with Delta One, Premium Select, First and Economy prices and seats left. Fares that mix cabins show each flight's cabin.
    • The month picker for calendar mode and hotel search now goes 14 months out.
  • 1.8.3Sep 29, 2026

    Multi-date fixes for Cathay, JAL and ANA

    • Cathay Pacific searches over several dates now return flights for every date, not just one.
    • JAL and ANA searches run one at a time per session, so dates searched together no longer overwrite each other.
  • 1.8.1Sep 29, 2026

    A better date picker

    • Type dates straight into the field, like 10/1-10/5 or 12/28-1/3. Dates without a year roll forward.
    • Arrow keys move between days, Enter picks and Esc closes. Click the month title to jump to another month.
    • Alaska fares that mix cabins (say, domestic first plus long-haul business) are listed under the fare's cabin, with each flight's cabin shown.
    • Booking I Prefer hotels with Choice points doesn't need a Choice login, so the login tip is gone.
  • 1.7.1Sep 28, 2026

    Recent searches, kept across reloads

    • The panel remembers your last search's dates, month range, and Search or Calendar mode, and the month range for hotels. Dates already past are skipped.
    • The last search's results stay in the panel when you reload the page (in the same tab).
    • New Recent searches list: pick one of your last 10 searches to run it again.
    • Cathay Pacific shows the right miles for each flight and cabin, including mixed-cabin itineraries.
  • 1.6.0Sep 27, 2026

    I Prefer hotel search

    • New hotel program: I Prefer (Preferred Hotels & Resorts) on iprefer.com and preferredhotels.com.
    • Two search modes: I Prefer points, or Choice Privileges points at the 300+ hotels that take them. Choice's taxes and fees are shown in cash.
    • Find hotels by name, code, city, state or country, or pick them from the page you're on.
  • 1.5.1Sep 27, 2026

    More reliable EVA Air search

    • Requests are spaced out and cached to avoid bot-check blocks. The panel now says when a session needs refreshing.
    • Searching starts after you've run one search by hand in the tab.
    • Works with EVA's new date header format.
  • 1.5.0Sep 27, 2026

    Choice Privileges hotel search

    • Points per night by month, with rows that link to the hotel with the points rate picked.
    • Find hotels by name, city or airport (nearest first), from the page, or by code.
  • 1.4.0Sep 27, 2026

    World of Hyatt hotel search

    • Points for every room type with award space (Standard, Club Access, Standard and Premium Suite), with off-peak / peak level.
    • A Room filter keeps one room type only.
  • 1.3.0Sep 27, 2026

    Hilton Honors hotel search

    • Lowest points per night with rooms left, including Premium Room Rewards when they're the lowest.
    • A Reward filter keeps Standard or Premium nights only.
  • 1.2.0Sep 27, 2026

    Marriott Bonvoy hotel search

    • Lowest points per night, with rows linking to the hotel's rate calendar.
  • 1.1.0Sep 27, 2026

    Hotel search, starting with IHG One Rewards

    • New hotel panel: points per night over a range of months, on a calendar and in a sortable table.
    • Hotel picker: search by name, city or airport, pick from the page, or type a hotel code.
  • 1.0.0Sep 26, 2026

    First release

    • Award flight search across many routes and dates on Alaska, American, Air Canada, ANA, Cathay Pacific, EVA Air, Flying Blue, Japan Airlines, LifeMiles and Starlux.
    • Calendar mode, Air Canada stopovers, JAL partner awards and the LifeMiles carrier filter.
    • Available as a userscript and a Chrome / Edge extension.
